# Dossier imagery

These are location photographs, not photographs of the portfolio owner.

Current Polaroids:

- `market.jpg`: **Carbon market dry goods section**, Headshop5, CC0 1.0.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Carbon_market_dry_goods_section.jpg
- `street.jpg`: **1955Walking taho street vendors in the Philippines 17**, Judgefloro, CC0 1.0.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:1955Walking_taho_street_vendors_in_the_Philippines_17.jpg

These are cropped at runtime with grayscale, contrast and simulated film grain.
They illustrate everyday life, not the identity of the portfolio owner.

Earlier retained source photos (not currently rendered):

- `cebu.jpg`: **Cebu city skyline 2025**, DisRaptor18, CC0 1.0.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cebu_city_skyline_2025.jpg
- `coast.jpg`: **Beach in Camarines Norte, Philippines, facing Pacific Ocean**, Acmekool, CC0 1.0.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Beach_in_Camarines_Norte,_Philippines,_facing_Pacific_Ocean.jpg
- `philippines.svg`: Generated from Natural Earth 1:50m country geometry, public domain.
  Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_50m_admin_0_countries.geojson
  Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
  Rebuild with `node scripts/dossier-map.mjs` (requires network).

Photos are stored locally at 960px, square-cropped and gently color-graded at runtime.
The map uses an equirectangular projection and a Cebu marker. The paper layout and
paper mounts are original procedural project assets. Metal clips have been removed.
