# CASE 404: Session notes, 2026-10-12

Branch `feat/board-contents`. What changed today, where it lives, and what is
still open. `docs/HANDOFF.md` remains the setup guide; `ARCHITECTURE.md` the
source of truth for structure.

## Evidence board

- **The case file is a 3D poster** at the board's centre, no longer painted on
  the map (`caseFile.poster` in `data/evidenceBoard.ts`,
  `paintCaseFileImage` in `surfaces/paintCaseBoard.ts`).
- **Role clusters.** All baked-in photos are hidden (`hiddenPieces`). Each
  role (Frontend, UI/UX, Graphic Design, Video Editing) has its note card and
  three 3D posters (landscape, portrait, square) using the wall poster models,
  with painted sample images (`roleClusters`, `paintEvidenceImage`).
- **Red strings.** Two hub pins sit above and below the case file. Each hub is
  strung to the file's edge and to two role cards, and each card to its
  posters. The strings sag naturally and are merged into one mesh. The pins
  are clones of an existing pin (`connections`).
- Poster images need `flipY = true` (the models' UVs run bottom-up).
- The painted board material is kept per board in a `WeakMap`
  (`paintedBoards` in `EvidenceBoard.tsx`), so the cached GLTF is never
  changed. This fixes a stale board texture after hot reload.

## Archive wall (computer side)

- **Achievements shelf** (`AchievementShelf.tsx`): a 1.54 m wood shelf,
  centred on the angled wall above the terminal (top at 1.56 m, clear of
  Wall-E). The plank is stretched along its length only.
- **Certificates.** Four framed certificates, two at each end, leaning on the
  wall. The certificate art is painted over each frame's own artwork
  (`surfaces/paintCertificate.ts`), and the text comes from
  `data/certificates.ts` (placeholders). No name is on them: the subject's
  name is revealed only in the dossier.
- **Bismarck display** (`ShipDisplay.tsx`): the KMS Bismarck model (0.54 m)
  in brass cradles on a walnut plinth with a brass name plate, in the middle
  of the shelf.
- **Inspect.** On the Graphic Design view, clicking a certificate or the ship
  brings the camera square-on to it. Back or Escape returns. This uses the new
  `focusFromFront` in `camera/focus.ts`; `useFocusable` now takes
  `view: "front"` and a `target`.
- **Design posters** sit lower, in two rows above the shelf (`DesignPosters.tsx`).
- **Camera.** The `posterWall` view is re-aimed to frame the shelf and both
  poster rows (target y 2.05).

## Merch corner

- **Mannequin** next to the board-side cabinet, turned toward the opening and
  merch cameras (manifest `mannequin`).
- **Case boxes** (`CaseBoxes.tsx`): the model is split into its six boxes and
  restacked:
  - one box on each cabinet;
  - a stack of three against the side wall;
  - an open box on the floor in front of the stack.
- Only lidded boxes carry another box. The open ones' flaps stand up past the
  rim, and a box set on them floated.
- **Box textures** are rebuilt from the source: colour 1024, normal 512,
  roughness and occlusion maps dropped (flat cardboard roughness). The fedora's
  colour and roughness maps went 1024 → 512 to stay inside the VRAM budget.

## Loader

- **New design** (`LoadingOverlay.tsx` + `.module.css`): the CASE 404 file
  under a desk lamp, with lamplight, flicker and film grain.
- **Progress.** A red string fills between two pins, with a typewriter counter
  and a status line that follows progress ("Dusting the desk for prints…").
- **Opening.** On ready, the cover swings open on its spine while the light
  swells, then the screen fades.
- **Reduced motion.** All loader motion is off; it only fades.
- **Scene rail** (`SceneNav`, new `hidden` prop) stays hidden until the scene
  is ready.
