# CASE 404 — Session notes, 2026-10-11

Branch `feat/new-comp`. What changed today, where it lives, and what is still
open. `docs/HANDOFF.md` remains the setup guide; `ARCHITECTURE.md` the source
of truth for structure.

## Fixes

- **Black scene on this machine.** The archive lamp started switched off and
  skipped its shadow pass, so its shadow map was never drawn. Stricter GPU
  drivers then rejected every draw call that sampled it
  (`GL_INVALID_OPERATION`), leaving only the lamp glow and smoke visible.
  Every lamp now renders its first few shadow frames (`DeskLampLight.tsx`).
  Likely also the cause of the old "blank until you scroll" note in HANDOFF.
- **Lamp hitbox too wide.** Dust motes, cigarette smoke and the bulb halo were
  clickable; three.js raycasts `Points` with a 1 m threshold, so each lamp had
  a ~2 m invisible hitbox. They now ignore the pointer (`raycast={() => null}`).
- **Polaroid prints flickering** (z-fighting with the desk): raised to 2 mm
  with 1.5 mm between prints, plus `polygonOffset`.

## Archive desk (computer)

- **Terminal screen** (`surfaces/paintArchive.ts`): larger, bolder text,
  lighter glow and scanlines, full-brightness screen material. Header stays
  fixed; the body scrolls, with a scrollbar and "SCROLL DOWN" hint.
- **Disks** (`diskStacks.ts`, `data/projects.ts`): nine disks in three spaced
  rows — Development, UI/UX and new Video Editing (purple "VIDEO" label).
  Hover lifts a disk straight up instead of sliding it sideways.
- **Insert sequence** (`ArchiveStation.tsx`): the screen keeps the menu until
  the disk reaches the drive (`INSERT_SECONDS` in `FloppyDisk.tsx`), then
  shows "DISK INSERTED / READING…" for ~1.5 s, then the file.
- **Video disks** play their clip on the CRT, tinted green, with a progress
  bar. Placeholder clip: `public/videos/sample-reel.mp4` (CC0, MDN). Set
  `video` per project in `data/projects.ts` to use real footage.
- **Click the screen to zoom in**; wheel, arrow keys, Page Up/Down, Home/End
  and touch scroll the terminal while zoomed.
- **Polaroid camera + three prints** (`PolaroidPhotos.tsx`, manifest entries
  `polaroidCamera`, `polaroidPhotos`): front-right of the desk. Optimised
  from `assets-source/` with `scripts/optimize-glb.mjs` (camera 44 MB → 1 MB,
  photos 1.1 MB → 0.3 MB). Click a print to look at it close.
- **Lighting**: a soft warm key light that fades in only at this desk, and a
  green CRT spill that exists only while the terminal is on
  (`StationLights` in `ArchiveStation.tsx`). Archive lamp strength 0.35.

## Lighting and room

- Both desk lamps stay on through the story and toggle by click
  (`lampState.ts`: `lampOn`, `toggleLamp`). Light, bulb glow, halo, dust and
  shadow pass fade together.
- Dust motes in each lamp's beam (`lighting/DustMotes.tsx`, GPU-only).
- Softer, slightly darker room: hemisphere 0.95 → 0.75, exposure 1.05 → 0.95,
  softer shadow radius; moonlight 1.8 → 1.55.
- Rounded corners where the angled walls meet the back and side walls
  (`CornerFillets` in `RoomShell.tsx`, `FILLET_RADIUS` 0.35). They use the
  plaster material with the manifest colour `#514b45` — copying the raw GLB
  material gave green strips. The plaster's UVs run one repeat per metre with
  **u up the wall and v along it**; `plasterUVs` maps code-built surfaces the
  same way. The right side wall is now plaster too.

## Cursor

  `useCursor.getState().set(kind, label)` or `hoverCursor(...)` — nothing
  sets `document.body.style.cursor` any more.
