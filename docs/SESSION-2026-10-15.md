## Wall sconces (replace the bar lamp over the board)

- **Removed:** the old bar lamp (`WallLamp.tsx`, `wall-lamp.glb`) and its two lights.
- **New sconces** (`WallSconces.tsx`): two brass twin-arm sconces either side
  of the board (x ±1.58, y 1.85); model `wall-sconce.glb`, manifest
  `wallSconce`.
- **Glass glow:** the glass shades glow with the "wall" lamp, through a
  glass-only emissive mask built from the model's colour map.
- **Switch:** click either sconce to switch both.
- **Their light comes from the bulbs** (`WallSconces.tsx`), per sconce:
  - a soft downward cone from the bells' mouths (`DOWN`), laying a scallop of
    light down the wall from the fitting;
  - a faint glow point in the bells (`GLOW_LIGHT`) for the light through the
    frosted glass.
- **Bounce fill** (`DetectiveOffice.tsx`): a soft point out in the room on each
  side, `[±1.0, 2.3, -2.65]` (left 1.8, right 1.5), standing in for light off
  the walls; it keeps the board readable and makes no pool on any wall.
- **Removed:** the raking spots that lit the board from in front of it, and the
  musket's picture light (both were light from nowhere).
- **Lamps off:** all six lights go dark, and the moonlight and its window
  shadows are unchanged.

## Mist

- `lighting/Mist.tsx`, mounted in `OfficeLighting`:
  - a faint haze through the room's depth (`FogExp2`, `#2b2723`, 0.065);
  - seven large soft wisps drifting and turning slowly, warm by the lamps,
    cool by the window, 6.5–10% opacity.
- **Colour:** the fog is the room's lit grey, not black, so it reads as air
  catching the light.
- **Reduced motion:** the wisps hold still.
- **Cost:** measured as nothing above noise (idle frame time and load).

## Musket display

- **What and where** (`MusketDisplay.tsx`, `old-musket.glb`, manifest
  `oldMusket`): a 1.5 m old flintlock musket lying level on two brass wall
  pegs, just above the board (about 2.17–2.35 m), centred on it. It faces left:
  muzzle left, butt right.
- **Orientation:** the model is exported on its side (its up is +z, with its butt
  at -x and muzzle at +x), so it is stood up and turned in one rotation
  (`[-PI/2, PI, 0]`, order `YXZ`). The pegs sit under its barrel and under the
  stock before the lock, at heights measured from the mesh.
- **Material:** one material for wood and iron, so metalness is forced to 0.5 (iron
  rendered black with no environment map) and the colour lifted ×1.3.
- **Lighting:** lit by the sconces' bounce fill only (its picture light was
  removed as light from nowhere).
- **Inspect:** click it for a front-on close-up during `CASE_OPENED` and
  `EVIDENCE_BOARD`.
- **Replaced the M1 Garand,** which was here first (`RifleDisplay.tsx`, removed
  with its model). The Garand's texture room came from deleting the unused
  `furniture/file-rack.glb`; the musket then took the Garand's room (colour map
  1536, others 512). `assets-source/realistic_m1_garand_updated.glb` is no
  longer used.
