## Archive terminal: disk sounds and eject transition

- **Sounds** (`audio/computerAudio.ts`, Web Audio synthesis, no files): insert
  is a pick-up tap, scrape, seating thunk and latch, then a motor with head
  seeks for the read time and a ready blip. Eject is the button click, spring
  pop and ring, scrape out, motor wind-down, a static burst and a tap in the
  tray. Timings follow `INSERT_SECONDS` so they land with the disk. Swapping
  disks plays an eject (no static) then an insert. Power-off and chapter-leave
  ejections stay silent.
- **Transition** (`ArchiveStation.tsx`, `paintTransition`): on eject the record
  tears into sliding bands with a sweeping bright band and static that peaks at
  the midpoint, where it swaps to the menu (750 ms, throttled to `PAINT_MS`, no
  `ctx.clip()`). Skipped when reduced motion is on.

- **Lag fixes** (measured; idle frames ~14 ms in every chapter, was 21-28; a playing video ~14 ms, was ~100):
  - The CRT texture is uploaded as plain RGBA and decoded from sRGB in its shader. sRGB canvas uploads cost ~80 ms of GPU time each. From a distance the text is ~8% dimmer.
  - Shadow maps render on demand (`canvas/ShadowDriver.tsx`, `canvas/shadowUpdates.ts`). Anything that moves and casts a shadow calls `shadowsChanged()`; new geometry or textures also trigger a redraw. A new moving prop must call it too, or its shadow will freeze.
  - Desk props: the instant camera and the phone are opaque and the magnifier lens is single pass. Transparent double-sided materials were drawn twice with per-frame program rebuilds.
  - The CRT repaints only while a clip moves.

  - Load time (14-33 s to scene-ready here).
  - The prod JS heap grew to 359-460 MB (not investigated).
