## Wall sconces (replace the bar lamp over the board)

- **Removed:** the old bar lamp (`WallLamp.tsx`, `wall-lamp.glb`) and its two lights.

- **Bounce fill** (`DetectiveOffice.tsx`): a soft point out in the room on each
  side, `[±1.0, 2.3, -2.65]` (left 1.8, right 1.5), standing in for light off
  the walls; it keeps the board readable and makes no pool on any wall.
- **Removed:** the raking spots that lit the board from in front of it, and the
  musket's picture light (both were light from nowhere).
- **Lamps off:** all six lights go dark, and the moonlight and its window
  shadows are unchanged.

## Mist

- `lighting/Mist.tsx`, mounted in `OfficeLighting`:
  - a faint haze through the room's depth (`FogExp2`, `#2b2723`, 0.065);
  - seven large soft wisps drifting and turning slowly, warm by the lamps,
    cool by the window, 6.5–10% opacity.
- **Colour:** the fog is the room's lit grey, not black, so it reads as air
  catching the light.
- **Reduced motion:** the wisps hold still.
- **Cost:** measured as nothing above noise (idle frame time and load).

## Musket display

- **What and where** (`MusketDisplay.tsx`, `old-musket.glb`, manifest
  `oldMusket`): a 1.5 m old flintlock musket lying level on two brass wall
  pegs, just above the board (about 2.17–2.35 m), centred on it. It faces left:
  muzzle left, butt right.
- **Orientation:** the model is exported on its side (its up is +z, with its butt
  at -x and muzzle at +x), so it is stood up and turned in one rotation
  (`[-PI/2, PI, 0]`, order `YXZ`). The pegs sit under its barrel and under the
  stock before the lock, at heights measured from the mesh.
- **Material:** one material for wood and iron, so metalness is forced to 0.5 (iron
  rendered black with no environment map) and the colour lifted ×1.3.
- **Lighting:** lit by the sconces' bounce fill only (its picture light was
  removed as light from nowhere).
- **Inspect:** click it for a front-on close-up during `CASE_OPENED` and
  `EVIDENCE_BOARD`.
- **Replaced the M1 Garand,** which was here first (`RifleDisplay.tsx`, removed
  with its model). The Garand's texture room came from deleting the unused
  `furniture/file-rack.glb`; the musket then took the Garand's room (colour map
  1536, others 512). `assets-source/realistic_m1_garand_updated.glb` is no

## Dossier: a stack of sheets with index tabs

- **Sheets** (`data/dossier.ts`, `surfaces/paintDossierPages.ts`): Case info,
  Education, Experience and Achievements, one on top at a time.
- **Tabs** (`DossierTabs.tsx`): paper divider tabs on the stack's right edge,
  labelled down the tab; they slide out as the folder finishes opening, the
  open sheet's is pulled out further and red. Click one (a raycast mesh), or
  use the left and right arrow keys. State: `useDossier` in `dossierState.ts`.
- **Changing sheet** (`Dossier.tsx`): the new sheet falls from above, tilted,
  and bounces onto the stack (`slamHeight`); the left cover's two photos and
  record card (`DossierEvidence.tsx`) swap 120 ms later and slam down in turn.
- **Painting ahead:** the other sheets and left-cover items are painted in idle
  time once the dossier is open (`whenIdle`, with a deadline, because a bare
  `requestIdleCallback` can wait forever on a page that renders every frame;
  that left them unpainted and made the first visit to a sheet hitch by
  0.4–0.9 s). Folding the dossier shut resets to the first sheet.
- **Type hierarchy** (`paintDossierPages.ts`): employer or school (large bold
  serif) > title (italic, muted) > body (regular) > labels, dates and heads
  (small tracked typewriter caps). Case info is two ruled tables under red
  section heads; Education and Experience are entries set apart by hairline
  dividers, with an "END OF RECORD" line under the last (a timeline rail with
  dots was tried and removed, and so was grouping the two NTV360 posts under one
  employer: each post is an ordinary entry). No page number at a sheet's foot.
  Skill items are held together with non-breaking spaces.
- **Experience entries** (`data/experience.ts`) take optional `summary`,
  `bullets` and `tools`, shown on the sheet.
- **Left-cover photos:** the board's eight street photos, two per sheet (never
  its two mugshots).
- **This replaced** an earlier four-page version (an on-screen tab strip at the
  foot of the screen, with the same drop), removed at the user's request.

## Archive terminal: disk sounds and eject transition

- **Sounds** (`audio/computerAudio.ts`, Web Audio synthesis, no files): insert
  is a pick-up tap, scrape, seating thunk and latch, then a motor with head
  seeks for the read time and a ready blip. Eject is the button click, spring
  pop and ring, scrape out, motor wind-down, a static burst and a tap in the
  tray. Timings follow `INSERT_SECONDS` so they land with the disk. Swapping
  disks plays an eject (no static) then an insert. Power-off and chapter-leave
  ejections stay silent.
- **Transition** (`ArchiveStation.tsx`, `paintTransition`): on eject the record
  tears into sliding bands with a sweeping bright band and static that peaks at
  the midpoint, where it swaps to the menu (750 ms, throttled to `PAINT_MS`, no
  `ctx.clip()`). Skipped when reduced motion is on.

## Music on page load

- `main.tsx` calls `gramophone.autoplay()`: the music starts as the page loads
  where the browser allows sound, otherwise on the first click, key press or
  tap (a wheel scroll does not count as an interaction to a browser). It fades
  in over 2.5 s with the record at full speed (no needle thump or pitch
  ramp, which need the scene's frame loop); clicking the gramophone still
  turns it off and on. A click on the gramophone that is the visitor's first
  interaction is not read twice (`SAME_CLICK_MS`).
- There is no sound button in the page UI: the gramophone is the only switch.
